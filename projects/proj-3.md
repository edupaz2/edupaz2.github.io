title: 'The Spengler Cup'
---
A hockey card game for the Spengler Cup: collect cards, play the daily quiz, make play-money predictions and learn hockey, all built on the real tournament data. It's an installable web app (PWA) with no accounts and no backend, and it was our team's entry to the HC Davos / Spengler Cup data challenge 2026.

Source code: [github.com/edupaz2/the-spengler-cup-2026](https://github.com/edupaz2/the-spengler-cup-2026)
