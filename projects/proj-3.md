Spengler Advent: a daily hockey door for the Spengler Cup. Each day brings five questions, play-money predictions and collectible cards, all built on the real tournament data. It's an installable web app (PWA) with no accounts and no backend, and it was our team's entry to the HC Davos / Spengler Cup data challenge.

Source code: [github.com/edupaz2/sic-data-hack-2026](https://github.com/edupaz2/sic-data-hack-2026)
